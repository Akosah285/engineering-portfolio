## Featured problem statements

Featured problems on this site are paraphrased in the author's voice from
Dartmouth coursework, with attribution in the form *"Adapted from Dartmouth
ENGS XX, [term]"*. Verbatim quotation occurs only where wording is mathematically
essential, in which case it is italicized and explicitly attributed.
